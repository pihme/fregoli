Chronicles

The history of Fregoli as set down so far, newest first.

September 2026

In the ninth month of the year of our Lord 2026, upon the twenty-fifth day, the maker set down the first words of Fregoli: a spec, bound under the PolyForm Noncommercial license. That same evening its house was raised upon GitHub. Scarce half an hour had passed ere the first slice stood complete: a Node and Cordis CLI bearing web and assistant plugins, a stub agent, a plugin loader, and the Grok CLI joined to it by ACP. Soon after came the page bridge, an image by which Fregoli might dwell within Hermetarium, and MCP tools wherewith the agent could behold the page and load plugins. At 20:47 v0.1.0 went forth with docs, CI and the tagging of releases, and ere the night was out five more followed it: v0.2.0 brought the chat-style assistant panel and the listing and unloading of plugins, v0.3.0 made reload_page keep the assistant open with its history, and v0.3.1 brought forth the welcome canvas. Upon the twenty-sixth day the scribes made a documentation pass, setting README, spec and usage guide in accord with the code; and it came to pass that this website was raised upon GitHub Pages, and before that day was done it had gained a sidebar, a drawing of its architecture, and these chronicles. Upon that same day the dependency warden Dependabot was set to watch over it, and by its hand the image was raised from node:22-alpine to node:26-alpine and the build brought to TypeScript 7; at 15:27 v0.3.2 went forth to carry these into a release.
